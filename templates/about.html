About Medivya

A question-answering assistant that reads medical reference material before it replies.

How it works

  1. Your question is converted into an embedding and matched against a library of medical documents.
  2. The three most relevant passages are retrieved from the Pinecone vector index.
  3. A Gemini language model writes an answer using those passages as context.

This approach is called retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It keeps answers tied to the source documents instead of relying on the model's memory alone.

What it can help with

General explanations of conditions, symptoms, medicines and treatments, written in plain language.

What it cannot do

It cannot diagnose, prescribe, or assess an emergency. If something feels urgent, contact a doctor or emergency services.

Technology

Flask, LangChain, Hugging Face embeddings, Pinecone and Google Gemini.
